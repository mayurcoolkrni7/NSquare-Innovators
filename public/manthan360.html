About Manthan 360

The board reads your DAF. Your prep should too.

A large part of the UPSC Personality Test grows out of your Detailed Application Form: where you studied, where you come from, where you've worked and what you do in your free time.

Manthan 360 reads your DAF, lets you confirm the details, and pulls the past interview questions that match them, so your limited time before the interview goes to what's most relevant.
